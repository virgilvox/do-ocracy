DO-OCRACY

Whoever does it, decides. Nobody does the dishes. One Tuesday night of Hack Your Hackerspace, up to 8 members on gamepads, keyboard and phones.

Controls

Pads, keys, phones

Pad
Stick or d-pad moves. A does it (hold for work). B drops. X proposes. Start begins the night.
Keys 1
Arrows, X or Space (A), Z (B), C (X), Enter (Start)
Keys 2
WASD, F (A), G (B), H (X)
Keys 3
IJKL, U (A), O (B), P (X)
Phone
Scan the QR on screen, or open the phone controller and type the room code.
The rules

Clout vs karma

CLOUT comes from the laser, the printers and the bench, shown off at Show & Tell. KARMA comes from dishes, trash, the Free Pile, fixing things and saying hi to newbies. CLUTTER at 100 brings the fire marshal. VIBE at 0 is burnout. If the space closes, all clout is void.

A wasmcart cart: three.c for the 3D, Bellows for the sound, CLASP for the phones. The same .wasc runs here, in RetroArch, and on the shop's game station.
