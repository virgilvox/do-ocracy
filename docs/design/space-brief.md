# HeatSync Labs as a space: a brief for the DO-OCRACY floor plan

108 W Main St, Mesa, Arizona 85201. 3,200 square feet, a storefront in downtown Mesa, continuously open since 2009, at this address since the 2018 move one building east from 140 W Main. No staff. Everything below is tagged.

Tags used:

- VERIFIED (url): a public page says it, quoted or paraphrased closely.
- PHOTO (file): seen in the lab's own photographs on heatsynclabs.org. The source files are in the website repo at `new-hsl/public/space-pics/` and `new-hsl/public/pics/`. Converted copies used for this brief are in `scratchpad/pics/` and `scratchpad/pics/site/`.
- HISTORICAL: true of the old building at 140 W Main (2011 to 2018) or of a specific past year. Use as lore, not as a current fact.
- PLAUSIBLE: a reasonable reading, not confirmed.
- LORE: game invention or general hackerspace culture, fine to keep, do not present as fact.

Orientation used throughout: even numbers are on the north side of Main Street (the Nile Theater is 105 W Main, across the street). The front door faces south onto Main Street, the back door opens north onto the alley. The wiki confirms the back door orientation indirectly: "Dumpsters and recycling: out the back door to the left (West) 225 feet down the alley" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Walk_Through_Orientation). Standing on the sidewalk facing the shop, left is west and right is east. The game's camera looks at the room from the street side, so screen left is west and screen right is east.

## 1. Street and storefront

- Downtown Mesa retail strip. In 2014 the AP described the lab as "a nondescript garage-like workshop nestled between restaurants, a flower shop and jewelry stores along Main Street" (VERIFIED, https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/?p=1163755, written about 140 W Main; the current block is the same character). Visitors in 2015 noted it is "one of very few hackerspaces we've seen over the past few weeks that gets foot traffic" and "located in a retail area in downtown Mesa" (VERIFIED, https://shop.keyboard.io/blogs/news/day-18).
- The building: "a larger, mid-century downtown storefront", moved into with help from RAILmesa and a $25,000 Made on Main Street grant from OneMain Financial and Main Street America; the grant list included "preservation-appropriate building improvements, tools and equipment, tables, chairs, instructor time, community engagement efforts, and a mural" (VERIFIED, https://mainstreet.org/the-latest/news/made-on-main-supporting-innovative-community-transformation-projects, January 2019). The move was described as letting "the workshop double in size" (VERIFIED, https://mainstreet.org/the-latest/news/community-spotlight-final-round-of-made-on-main-street-events, October 2018). The 2018 move needed fire sprinklers, a fire riser cut in from the water main in the alley, electrical work and a structural engineer "to rate upstairs floor" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/108_moving_needs).
- The front is a classic mid-century recessed storefront: two large plate-glass display windows angled in toward a recessed vestibule, aluminium-framed glass double doors in the centre, a dark (near-black) fascia with a backlit white "HeatSync Labs" sign with the mark above the doors, recessed downlights in the soffit, and a speckled brown terrazzo-style tile in the vestibule (PHOTO: Entrance.jpg, site/p17.jpg, site/p9.jpg). The glass door carries the mark and "108" (PHOTO: site/p9.jpg). The right window has white vinyl: "HeatSync Labs. Hacking Arizona Since 2009" (PHOTO: site/p17.jpg). The wishlist asks for "New vinyl for front sign 12' x 14"" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Wishlist).
- An A-frame sign goes out on the sidewalk during open hours and comes in at close (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Hosting). A yellow laser-cut donation box has stood out front at events (PHOTO: site/p17.jpg).
- The display windows are curated show cases with white pegboard backs and track lighting. West window: a wall of small CRT televisions playing demos on black cubes, yellow cube shelving lit with LEDs, HeatSync posters, a Mesa bike QR poster (PHOTO: CRT_Wall.jpg, site/p6.jpg). East window: a toy humanoid robot, a 4x4x4 LED cube, a networked LED marquee panel, a laser-cut sign reading DIVERSE INCLUSIVE ACCEPTING WELCOMING SAFE SPACE FOR EVERYONE, a DEFCON banner, an "AZ Haunters" tombstone, an Arduino on a breadboard driving a "Welcome to" LCD, a jewelry display, two screens showing DONATE with a QR code and the wordmark (PHOTO: Front_Window.jpg, Front_window_2.jpg, Window_Decor.jpg, All_are_welcome.jpg). The window screens run PiSignage; the older GitHub Pages signage is retired (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Streamlining_Engagement and https://github.com/heatsynclabs/digitalsignage). The marquee panel is the one `marquee.html` in the heatsync skill drives (VERIFIED, heatsync skill, references/lab-tools.md).
- A microphone was once dropped through a drilled hole in the roof to the outside so the window display could react to passers-by, with a mannequin wearing a tablet on its head (HISTORICAL, 2016, https://iceddev.com/blog/putting-a-face-on-the-local-lab/).

What this means for the game: the whole front edge is glass. The door is in the centre, recessed, with a show window either side. "Show and Tell in the front window" is correct in spirit; the real windows are permanent curated displays, not a stage.

## 2. Just inside the door: the front hall

- The WiFi password is "above the front doors below the exit sign in green vinyl lettering" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). A red LED clock and an EXIT sign sit above the inside of the doors (PHOTO: Coworking.jpg, site/p15.jpg). Fire extinguisher by the front door; the facility manager's phone number is in the bottom corner of the front door sign (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation).
- Hosting rule: the host sits "somewhere near the front of the lab, and helping anybody that comes in" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Open_Hours) and is to "be near enough to the front doors that you will notice if new people come into the lab" (VERIFIED, Hosting). "There is no one whose job it is to sit and greet people coming in the door (front or back). There is a host... They may sometimes wear a captain's hat, but mostly they look like everyone else there" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/New_to_HeatSync_Labs%3F). Liability waivers are "online or at the front computer" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). A guest sign-in book was proposed in 2012 (HISTORICAL).
- The front of the lab is the clean end. The 2015 tour guide's line: the lab "goes from clean to dirty" with "worktables, a wall of microcontrollers, a few workstations, a projector and a couple 3D printers" at the front, then "through the electronics area into the lasercutting area", then "the machine shop, which has everything from CNC mills to a lens-grinding setup" (VERIFIED, keyboard.io, about 140 W Main). Hackaday 2013: walking in "puts you directly into a large open gathering area", past it "a few rows of workbenches with bench power supplies, oscilloscopes, and soldering and rework stations", "a large laser cutter sits in the back corner", and a back room keeps "messier work" separate: "woodworking, machine tools, and welding" (VERIFIED, https://hackaday.com/2013/03/11/hackerspace-intro-heatsync-labs/, about 140). The current building follows the same gradient (PHOTO set).
- The signature view: a long row of plywood coworking desks with monitors and black mesh office chairs running along the east wall from the front toward the back, under large white letters reading "hack" on an amber band, with big dark-grey dome pendant lamps hanging on yellow cords (PHOTO: Coworking.jpg, Main_Hall.jpg, site/p15.jpg). "Co-working tables with monitors" were a member donation (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Other_Resources). The WiFi password is also "on all the co-working monitors" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/signage).
- The middle of the front hall holds big plywood-topped tables on trestle legs: a meeting table with laser-cut QR signs ("Hack Your Hackerspace 7pm", "Young Makers"), an orange bucket, a blue bin under it, a bell jar; more tables with white tops and black chairs further in (PHOTO: Main_Hall.jpg). HYH minutes are shown "on the projector in the centre of the room" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/HYH_Meeting_Template, 2012; the A/V station still has a projector and a wireless mic, VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/AudioVisual). LEGO Day happens at a table right by the front doors (PHOTO: site/p13.jpg).
- Bylaws list "not closing the shop door during a meeting or class up front" as inconsiderate (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Bylaws), so there is a door between the front (meetings and classes) and the shop (noise).

## 3. The station bays along the walls

The main hall walls are divided into bays, each with white pegboard, an amber band above at bulkhead height, an LED strip under the band lip, and most with an orange sign tile. "Most of the stations have an orange tile with the name of the station made with black vinyl" (VERIFIED, signage). Tiles seen: LASER CUTTER, WOOD SHOP, LOCK PICK, VINYL STATION, 3D PRINTER (PHOTO: Laser_Cutters.jpg, Woodshop.jpg, Front_window_2.jpg, site/p11.jpg). Many tools carry QR codes linking the manual (VERIFIED, signage). Some tools wear a sticker "Notification HeatSync Labs Training Required" (VERIFIED, signage). "We have donation boxes set up at most of the workstations" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Joining_In); "the tall donation bin between electronics and the woodworking tools area" (VERIFIED, signage).

The library catalogue was last updated 2013 (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Library). "Weekly Caffeine and Coworking, Wednesdays 9am to 3pm" is the oldest running program (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Events; hackerspace-forge 07-heatsync-dataset).

"A wall of microcontrollers available for hacking projects" (VERIFIED, keyboard.io).

- 3D printing: a long black steel shelving unit, printers on the middle shelf, filament spools lined up on top, a resin printer and filament dryer below, a chair and computer at the end (PHOTO: 3D_Printing_Station.jpg). ## 4. The laser station

- Two red Chinese CO2 lasers: the Large 80W Laser (cutting area 1199 x 769 mm, "48"(w) x 32"(d)", a rear door flap for long stock, runs LaserCut 5.3 with a SoftDog dongle, needs the chiller, exhaust fan and air compressor on) and the Mini 60W Laser (75 x 65 cm bed, LightBurn, manual Z knob, "the switches on the side are where you actually need to turn it on") (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Large_80W_Laser and https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Mini_60W_Laser). The wiki's own names are "Large 80W Laser" and "Mini 60W Laser"; the big one wears a decal "HeatSync Labs. Hacking Arizona Since 2009. CO2 laser 80w" (PHOTO: Laser_Cutters.jpg). No public nickname like PEWPEW 9000 exists (LORE).
- The bay: the 80W on the left, a computer desk with a yellow PC tower between, the 60W on a rolling cart, a second workstation, pegboard full of laser-cut samples (a clock, a wreath, gears, the mark), posters "BE NICE. BE MAKING. CLEAN UP.", a "Laser Cutter Rules" board, the orange LASER CUTTER tile, a silver flex exhaust duct running up and along the wall (PHOTO: Laser_Cutters.jpg, site/p16.jpg).
- Rules: certified only; "Always stay in the area when your job is running"; "Clean up after your job"; "Log your work when you are done"; "Empty the trash out in the dumpster when it is 3/4 full"; "Take turns with others"; "Post a sign on the laser if it's out of order" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Laser_Cutters). Materials: acrylic, plywood, MDF, cardboard, paper, leather, fabric; never PVC, polycarbonate, PTFE, metal, "skin/flesh" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Laser_Material_Guidelines). Laser Certification is the 4th Friday 7 to 10, Laser Meetup the 2nd Sunday (VERIFIED, Events).
- "Thermostat: doorway by laser into hallway", "Fire extinguishers: under thermostat near the laser" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). So the laser sits where the hall narrows into the back hallway.
- Laser scrap and material storage beside it: "The storage area for the materials used at the laser is a disaster"; a proposal for plywood bays painted green, yellow, orange, red so scrap cycles weekly toward the trash, "It's HeatSync Labs, not HeatSync storage" (VERIFIED as a proposal, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Laser_storage_proposal; whether it was built is PLAUSIBLE). The 2021 cleanup found material with "parking pass dates of 2017 and had been red-tagged in 2019" and a pile "over by the chair corral/can crusher" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/2021_Laser_storage_cleanup).

## 5. The hallway, kitchen, stairs, bathrooms

Behind the laser the building narrows into a hallway cluster. Sources place these things in it:

- "Safety glasses: hung on the wall outside the metal shop"; "Vehicle parking passes: next to safety glasses"; "Light switches: in hall near metal shop"; "First aid kit and eye wash: in hallway in front of small bathroom door"; "Fire extinguisher: in hallway"; "Light switches: bottom of stairs for upstairs lights"; "Upstairs storage/IT" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). Hosts point newcomers to "restrooms, First Aid (next to bathroom)" (VERIFIED, Hosting). The bathroom dispensers take a specific foaming soap refill (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/supplies).
- Kitchen: "MSDS / Safety Data Sheets: in the kitchen in the window"; "Cleaning supplies: in the kitchen on the rack and in the cupboards next to the rack" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). "Maintenance Supply Closet: located beside the stairs, next to the kitchen. Stores our brooms, mops, and other cleaning equipment. The drain pipe in this closet leads, of course, to the sewer... sometimes it can dry out, allowing foul-smelling sewer gases to leak into the lab. If this happens, pour about 6 cups of water into the drain" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/HeatSync_Labs_Space). A kitchen sink is PLAUSIBLE (not stated; the biochem lab has a utility sink, PHOTO). The game's dish mechanic is LORE in HeatSync terms, though "clearing tables of food or drink" is a hosting duty (VERIFIED, Hosting).
- The darkroom "is located in the kitchen. The Darkroom is not a permanent fixture" and uses "2x Reflectix window covers" and a "Darkroom in use sign" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/darkroom). The photo shows a Beseler enlarger against an orange-painted wall with the HeatSync mark painted large in black, foil window covers taped over a window, and wire shelving of chemicals (PHOTO: Dark_Room_Photography.jpg). This orange wall with the painted mark is the closest thing to a mural that any source shows; the exterior mural promised in the 2019 grant write-up is not confirmed (PLAUSIBLE at best).
- Upstairs: "The space itself is a former retail store, long and narrow and the small upstairs is the former storage and office area" with "a very inaccessible staircase" (VERIFIED as a comment about 140 W Main, hackaday 2012). At 108: upstairs holds storage and IT, the pfSense firewall "running upstairs, labelled hsl-firewall" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Network, 2012 era), screen printing parts "stored upstairs to prevent damage to the screens", art supplies including "40 easels", a pad printer "that is upstairs because it wasn't getting used"; "Upstairs is limited access so someone hosting should assist" (VERIFIED, Screen_Printing, Arts_and_Crafts, Other_Resources). HYH chores include "Review upstairs storage" (VERIFIED, Hack_Your_Hackerspace). A recording studio upstairs "which doubles as a camera obscura" (HISTORICAL, keyboard.io, 140 W Main).
- Soda history: a vending machine at the front and a back "kitchen" area, restocked from a Kalil account, an honour jar "above the fridge", members asking that "Monster Rehab Protean" never be ordered again (HISTORICAL, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Soda, addressed to 140 W Main; and a 2012 HYH where someone "would rather it be full of bizarre/interesting things, not snacks"). Today a snack fridge with an honour box sits by the sewing station (PHOTO). There is no Club-Mate in any HeatSync source (LORE).

## 6. The machine shop

- A separate room off the hallway with its own door. Bare sealed concrete floor, stained and chipped; grey walls; fluorescent tube fixtures on a low painted ceiling; a yellow and black caution tape line on the floor in front of the electrical panel with a CAUTION sign above it; orange Home Depot buckets; grey lockers; a red tool cart; a blue steel bench (PHOTO: Machine_Shop.jpg, Drill_Press.jpg, site/p18.jpg).
- Tools: a green Central Machinery 12 x 36 lathe, a blue manual mill with a DRO ("The Blue Mill. Parts of a Sheldon and Rosnok (not 100% sure)"), a CNC mill ("Someone's DIY conversion from a manual mill to a CNC", Centroid CNC12, Gecko drives), a mini mill, a 7 x 12 mini lathe, a Grizzly drill press, a Delta bench grinder, a Rigid abrasive cutoff saw, a Jet horizontal/vertical bandsaw, a 3-in-1 shear/brake/roll, an air compressor, plate shears (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Machine_Shop and Tools). Certification for lathe, mill and CNC; "being certified on the blue manual mill is a prerequisite to being certified on the CNC mill"; "Do not run hand-edited G-code" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/CNC_Mill). The mill is not a Bridgeport, as the game says; that detail is right.
- Lore worth keeping: "TRUST NO ONE: verify tools, setup, and machine mounting yourself", "Remove chuck key immediately after use", "DO NOT STAND in front when turning on/off: shattering wheel can kill" (VERIFIED, heatsync skill governance/equipment-rules.md). "No Brake Cleaner in the lab!" because it makes phosgene with welding arcs (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation).

## 7. The welding room and the back door

- A back room with painted cinder-block walls (white, scuffed), a bare concrete floor, a welding table, blue welding curtains, a belt sander, gas cylinders chained in the corner, extension cords on hooks, and the back door: a green steel door with a paper sign "Turn Off Gas. Due to Fire Regulations All Hot Work Must be Done Outdoors" and "Turn Off Gas" stencilled twice on the wall (PHOTO: Welding.jpg). "No welding or torch work is allowed indoors. We do not have proper fire sprinkler system. Please use the welding table behind the lab" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Welding; note the 2018 sprinkler project, so this sentence may be older than the sprinklers). MIG (Lincoln Power MIG 140c) and TIG (Miller Dynasty 200 SD) (VERIFIED, Welding).
- Outside the back door: the alley. The building's back is red brick with the green door in a recess; welding classes happen on the asphalt right outside, the blue curtain wheeled out, cars parked beside (PHOTO: site/p14.jpg). "Dumpsters and recycling: out the back door to the left (West) 225 feet down the alley (use the wheelbarrow for trash). Padlock code for the dumpsters is on the exit sign above the back door" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). So there is no dumpster at the door; trash travels by wheelbarrow.
- Closing: "Doors should automatically lock at 10pm but make sure... Check the doors do in fact lock (front, back, and where welding stuff is)" (VERIFIED, Hosting). So there are three exterior doors: front, back, and the welding room's.
- In 2011 at the old lodge the back door "has never been available to us for regular entrance" (HISTORICAL). In 2012 members "usually have the back door opened so you can just park around back" (HISTORICAL, WordPress archive). Parking today: the Green Lot across North MacDonald, Green and Orange lots 3 hours max 8am to 8pm, the 30 W Pepper garage on weekends; street parking is 2 hours and ticketed (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation and operations-and-procedures.md).

## 8. The biochem lab

A small side room: white painted block walls, beige ceramic floor tile, a utility sink with exposed plumbing, stainless tables, two microscopes, an analytical balance, an incubator, a wine-fridge-sized sample fridge, a yellow flammables cabinet, an anatomical skeleton, a whiteboard with circuit doodles (PHOTO: Biohacking_Lab.jpg). ## 9. Surfaces, light, air

- Floor, main hall: VCT (vinyl composition tile). The wiki page is literally titled "Facilities Floor: Cleaning VCT Tile Flooring" and tells the whole saga: "We should clean the floors. The streak marks from chair legs and wheels look bad... Would distilled white vinegar work? What about muriatic acid? [testing ensues]... The lowest quote was $4,000. Why don't we just clean them ourselves? Return to step 1, above... There isn't a shortcut. To get these kinds of serious stains off, you have to strip. Once you strip, you have to seal and polish" (VERIFIED, https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Facilities_Floor). The game's attract-mode loop quotes this and should keep doing so.
- Floor pattern: 12-inch square tiles, mostly off-white with grey speckle, worn and scuffed, with runs of solid red tiles and solid orange tiles laid as rectangles and bands: a long red rectangle in the aisle beside the coworking desks, an orange L and orange strip through the hall, red and orange rectangles under the HYH table, a red rectangle in front of the laser bay and another in front of the 3D printers, an orange band through electronics, an orange stripe into the jewelry alcove, red patches by the sewing tables (PHOTO: Main_Hall.jpg, Coworking.jpg, Laser_Cutters.jpg, 3D_Printing_Station.jpg, Jewlery_Station.jpg, Sewing_Station.jpg, site/p19.jpg, site/p13.jpg). This is exactly the floor the user asked for and it is real. The rectangles are irregular, one to three tiles wide and three to twelve long, not a grid.
- Floor elsewhere: sealed concrete in the machine shop and welding room; beige ceramic tile in the biochem lab; brown terrazzo-style tile in the vestibule (PHOTO). The old building had "a hardwood floor" of "sickly yellow color" (HISTORICAL, Lab_Furnishings).
- Walls: painted mid-grey, with a horizontal amber/orange band at bulkhead height running around the main hall (the lip of a soffit above the bays), big white "hack" letters on the east wall band, white pegboard in most bays, grey acoustic foam panels in rows high on the walls, exposed conduit and cable (PHOTO: Main_Hall.jpg, Coworking.jpg, Laser_Cutters.jpg). The front hall has a lower wainscot of dark grey painted tongue-and-groove under the window display shelf (PHOTO: Front_window_2.jpg). Interior walls are not brick. The only brick is the exterior back wall (PHOTO: site/p14.jpg).
- Ceiling: the main hall ceiling is a flat hard ceiling painted charcoal, with ceiling fans, surface-mounted fluorescent tube fixtures, pendant dome lamps on long yellow cords, a few bare bulb fixtures and a flex exhaust duct from the laser (PHOTO: Main_Hall.jpg, site/p8.jpg, site/p16.jpg). Not a drop ceiling, not exposed trusses. Back rooms have lower painted ceilings with fluorescent tubes (PHOTO: Machine_Shop.jpg, Biohacking_Lab.jpg).
- Lighting: dark grey dome pendants (IKEA Hektar style) in rows over the coworking desks and the hall; fluorescent tubes; LED strip under the amber soffit lip lighting the pegboard bays; ceiling fans; track lights in the display windows; recessed downlights in the entry soffit. No string lights in any photo (the game's string lights are LORE). Hosts "turn on enough lights that people don't think we are closed" (VERIFIED, Hosting). A space heater is checked at open and close (VERIFIED, Hosting).
- Climate: Mesa summers, the lab runs air conditioning, "Please do not set the thermostat at a temperature that will result in a high electric bill" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). Electricity is the second largest expense after rent (VERIFIED, operations-and-procedures.md).
- Cameras: three IP cameras, "Front of lab security coverage", "Front of lab live.heatsynclabs.org coverage" (VERIFIED, Network, 2012); "Webcams and video recording, no expectation of privacy" (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation). The website shows whether the doors are unlocked (VERIFIED, Open_Hours and https://www.heatsync.us/).

## 11. Rhythms

All VERIFIED from https://wiki.heatsynclabs.org/wiki/Events, the FAQ, https://www.heatsync.us/ and the heatsync skill unless noted.

- Open hours: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday 7pm to 10pm; Saturday noon to 5pm. Free, membership not required. Doors auto-lock at 10pm. Members with card access come any time; card access is "a privilege based on trust, not a right", earned by nomination and a vote at HYH after months of showing up (VERIFIED, Walk Through Orientation and Operational_Policies).
- Hack Your Hackerspace: 2nd Thursday 7pm and 4th Saturday (the FAQ says 9am for the Saturday). "HYH is the members' night to get stuff done around the space. It is not a discussion time." Vote first, quickly, quorum 8; then "Clean/fix/replace/action everything", new members get their boxes, review the lost and found and the free stuff shelf, check the broken tools bin, check "cups/toilet paper/cleaning products", review upstairs storage, process donations (VERIFIED, Hack_Your_Hackerspace). Proposals lock a week before.
- Weekly: Caffeine and Coworking, Wednesdays 9am to 3pm; Night Writers, Sundays 7 to 10; Perler Bead Craft Night, Mondays 8pm.
- Monthly: Craft Night 1st and 3rd Mondays; Jewelry Meetup 2nd and 4th Mondays; Arduino Night 1st Wednesday 7 to 10 ("No experience necessary"); Prusa User Group 1st Thursday; Google Developer Group 3rd Thursday; Young Makers 1st and 3rd Friday 6 to 10 ("No babysitting. No lectures."); Bicycle Maintenance 3rd Fridays; Laser Certification 4th Friday; Photo Meetup 4th Friday; LEGO Day 1st Saturday; 3D Printer Day 1st Sunday noon to 5; Laser Meetup 2nd Sunday; Design Day 3rd Sunday; Sewing Night 2nd Tuesday; OWASP Phoenix 1st Tuesday 8:30pm.
- Classes: instructors set the price, 70 percent to the instructor, 30 percent to the lab; certification classes for laser, lathe, mill, CNC, welder, table saw and router (VERIFIED, Events and WoodShop).
- Scale: 6,811 events and 25,177 scheduled hours over 2010 to 2025; 562 HYH runs; 1,716 hours of cleaning and maintenance; about 95 members in late 2025; rent and utilities about 83 percent of expenses (VERIFIED, hackerspace-forge 07-heatsync-dataset.md). "A hackerspace is mostly a janitorial co-op with classrooms attached" (VERIFIED, same file, and already a game tagline).
- Sleeping in the lab "is only acceptable for 2 nights per week or less" (VERIFIED, Operational_Policies).
- Season: Arizona. The game's IT IS 118 OUTSIDE and HABOOB events are LORE but true to place.

## 12. What the game currently gets wrong or misses

Compared with the README "The space" section and `LAYOUT[]` / `OBST[]` in `src/game.c`, plus `paint_floor()` and `build_room()` in `src/world.c`.

Wrong:

1. Floor. The game paints sealed concrete with an OSB wood-shop floor, a cream and char checkerboard kitchen, diamond plate in the metal shop and a purple lounge rug. The real main hall is white speckled VCT with red and orange tile rectangles everywhere; concrete exists only in the machine shop and welding room. There is no checkerboard anywhere. The user's request matches reality and the fix is also the most recognisable HeatSync surface.
2. Walls. The game draws brick with a char wainscot. Interior walls are painted grey with white pegboard bays and an amber band at bulkhead height; the only brick is the exterior back wall. Replace brick with grey plaster, pegboard and the amber band; keep the char wainscot only along the front window base.
3. Lighting. The game has fluorescent strips high on the walls and string lights. Real: dome pendants on yellow cords in rows, ceiling fluorescents, LED strip under the amber lip, ceiling fans. Drop the string lights.
4. Front door position. `DOOR_X` is -8 (far left). The real entry is a recessed centre vestibule with a display window either side. Put the door at x near 0 and make the two windows real show cases.
5. The alley door and the trash. The game puts a steel alley door mid-way along the right wall with a dumpster inside beside it. The real back door is in the welding room at the far back, the dumpster is 225 feet down the alley and trash goes by wheelbarrow. In the room's terms: trash leaves through the back door, the dumpster is off-map.
6. Metal shop on the open floor. It is a separate room with its own door, concrete floor and caution tape, not a lathe and mill against the right wall.
7. Kitchen and lounge at the front left. The kitchen is at the back by the stairs and the hallway (and doubles as the darkroom). The front left is the west show window, the LEGO table and the Coffee and Books corner. There is no front lounge; the couch, if kept, lives by the laser.
8. Wood shop as a walled zone with a lumber rack. "Not a dedicated area": a bay of benchtop tools on the main floor with white-topped tables; the big saws roll out back. The lumber lives in the laser's scrap bays with parking passes and red tags.
9. Names. PEWPEW 9000 and Loafer are inventions; public names are Large 80W Laser and Mini 60W Laser, and printers Ouch, Spidey, Dr. Strange (down, which the game already jokes about) and EnderDragon. Sir Cutsalot is real (spelled Sir-Cuts-A-Lot). Bob Ross is the colour laser printer. Either adopt the real names or keep the jokes and say so in the README.
10. Night timing. Open hours end at 10pm and the doors lock themselves; the game runs 7 to 11. HYH is the 2nd Thursday and the 4th Saturday, never Tuesday. If "Tuesday" is kept it is an ordinary open-hours night (and Sewing Night once a month), and the README should stop calling it HYH, or the night should become Thursday.
11. Club-Mate. Not a HeatSync thing in any source. The honour-system fridge holds soda, Monster and AZ iced tea, and there is a snack box. "Please never ever order Monster Rehab Protean again" is the real joke.
12. The bike on the wall. The real bike thing is a repair station in a black filing cabinet between the laser and member storage, with a bike stand.

Missing, and worth adding:

- The coworking row of desks and monitors along the east wall under the big "hack" letters. It is the most photographed view of the lab.
- The amber band with orange station tiles in black vinyl. GANTRY hazard amber on char, already the game's palette.
- The two display windows as show cases (CRT wall and yellow cubes on the west, robot, LED cube and marquee on the east) with the DONATE screens facing the street.
- Parking passes (green, 7 days) and red tags (14 days) as the clutter mechanic: a tagged project is tolerated, an untagged one is clutter, an expired tag goes to the free stuff shelf and then the trash.
- The hallway cluster: safety glasses on the wall outside the metal shop, first aid by the small bathroom door, the stairs up to a "limited access" upstairs, the supply closet whose drain smells when it dries out (pour six cups of water in it: a karma task).
- The welding room with the "Turn Off Gas" door as the way out to the alley.
- The A-frame sign out front at open, in at close; the lights on "so people don't think we are closed"; hot tools and failed prints checked at close.
- Member lockers and bankers boxes (the lost and found "collect your stuff shelf" behind the grey Tech Tear Apart bins).
- The jewelry alcove, sewing and the snack fridge, the music corner with Bob Ross, the biochem room, the LEGO yellow crates: colour and stations the real lab has that the game lacks.
- The floor-cleaning saga as the wall-clock joke it is (the game has the text; the floor should visibly be scuffed VCT so the joke lands).
